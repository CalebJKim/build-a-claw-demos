Start Here

FDIC | 35 min | Score 95/100

Money Smart for Adults

A modular financial education curriculum covering values, banking, income, expenses, spending plans, savings, credit, borrowing, housing, and more.

Use this as the backbone because it is broad, practical, and designed for people building confidence with everyday money decisions.

Khan Academy | 45 min | Score 91/100

Personal Finance

A free course with units on saving, budgeting, interest, debt, investing, retirement, income, housing, cars, taxes, college, and information safety.

Good for someone who wants short lessons and videos before reading denser reference material.

Budgeting and Cash Flow

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau | 25 min | Score 95/100

Your Money, Your Goals Toolkit

A toolkit of fillable worksheets and handouts for cash flow, bills, debt, credit reports, goals, and money conversations.

Start here when the learner needs to turn broad advice into a concrete weekly or monthly money plan.

Saving and Emergency Funds

TreasuryDirect | 15 min | Score 85/100

FAQs about Savings Bonds

A TreasuryDirect FAQ explaining the basics of Series EE and Series I savings bonds, purchase minimums, taxes, buying, and cashing.

Useful as an optional reference for safe savings instruments after emergency cash basics are covered.

Credit and Debt

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau | 20 min | Score 95/100

Credit Reports and Scores

A plain-language hub for understanding credit reports, credit scores, errors, disputes, and related consumer rights.

A beginner should understand reports before chasing score hacks or paid credit products.

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau | 15 min | Score 93/100

Understand Your Credit Score

A focused explanation of where credit scores come from, why there are many scores, and how lenders use them.

Read after the broader credit hub to avoid oversimplified myths about a single universal score.

Investing Basics

Investor.gov | 25 min | Score 93/100

Introduction to Investing

An SEC investor education page explaining major investment products and the fact that market investments can lose value.

It gives the right cautionary frame before a beginner moves from saving into investing.

FINRA | 35 min | Score 92/100

Investing Basics

A beginner investing guide covering securities, goals, risk, diversification, fees, performance, buying and selling, and volatility.

Use this to connect investing vocabulary to concrete decisions like time horizon, fees, and risk.

FINRA | 18 min | Score 91/100

Asset Allocation and Diversification

A focused guide to asset allocation, diversification, and rebalancing as tools for managing investment risk.

Read this once the learner knows the vocabulary and needs a simple mental model for portfolio risk.

Retirement

Internal Revenue Service | 25 min | Score 86/100

Saving for Retirement

An IRS hub for retirement saving basics, employer plans, contributions, fees, automatic enrollment, distributions, rollovers, and related tips.

Use it as a reference once the learner reaches workplace plans, IRAs, tax advantages, and retirement account rules.
